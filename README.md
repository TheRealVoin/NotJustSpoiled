Not Just Spoiled adds simple food spoilage mechanic. This mod enhances your food managment and perfectly fits in any modpack.

# Features
### Food status
Now all food will have 4 different statuses: fresh, stale, half-spoiled and spoiled. Each status has its own hunger and saturation parameters.

### Food category
The mod adds 25+ food categories with unique food spoilage time.

### Food environment
Currently Not Just Spoiled provides us 4 environments: inventory, storage, ground and while cooking. The worse the environment, the faster the food spoils!

# What about Fabric/NeoForge/older versions?
### Forge:
The only supported version is 1.20.1.
### Fabric/NeoForge:
It will be ported only after the mod is fully released, all core mechanics are implemented, and the bugs have been fixed.
### Older versions:
There is no plans to backport it to older minecraft versions.
